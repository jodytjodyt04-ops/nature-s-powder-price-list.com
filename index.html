Our process

Powder, made the patient way

Every batch starts as whole fruit or vegetable, washed and sliced by hand, then dried slowly at controlled temperatures until the moisture is gone but the colour and flavour stay behind. What's left is milled into a fine powder — ready to stir into food, drinks or supplements.
